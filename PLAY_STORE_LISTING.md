## Full description (max 4000 chars)

```
AkvoPura is the official ordering and delivery app for AkvoPura Mineral
Water, serving Timergara and Shergarh in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Pakistan.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

For customers:
• Self-register and pick your branch (Timergara or Shergarh)
• Choose your customer type at sign-up — refillable Cans/Gallons or
  Bottled Pets (600 ml / 1.5 L packs)
• Place new orders for water delivery any time
• See every bill, payment, and return in your account
• Track your outstanding balance and empty container deposit
• File complaints directly to your branch salesman or manager
• Subscribe to weekly recurring orders so you never run out

For salesmen (Cans/Gallons or Bottled Pets):
• Today's route with color-coded customer cards (white / yellow / red)
• Slide-to-confirm delivery with quantity steppers
• Separate empty-container collection sheet
• Auto-generated PDF bill — share via WhatsApp in one tap
• Live van load tracking so you know what you have left
• Undo any mistake within the same day

For branch managers:
• Approve customer registrations and configure their route, payment
  cycle, and price tier
• Manage all staff accounts at your branch (salesmen, production,
  drivers, helpers)
• See today's attendance, trips, and expense submissions
• Review and resolve complaints
• Track production batches and raw material inventory
• Order forecasting based on the previous week's deliveries

For the business owner:
• Two-branch dashboard with daily cash, sales, debt, and inventory
• Comparison view between branches
• Salesman leaderboard
• Aging report for debt collection
• Audit log of every staff action
• Manage branch managers — add, edit, deactivate, reset password
• Set default prices for cans, gallons, and bottles
• Set fees for lost or damaged containers

WHY AKVOPURA

• Built specifically for Pakistani water delivery businesses
• Works on slow networks — every transaction syncs as soon as you have
  signal
• Defensive money math — no penny can disappear from a customer's balance
• Bill PDFs are auto-generated and emailed to the business owner for
  archive
• Designed for big tap targets and easy use without typing

HOW TO GET STARTED

Customers: open the app, tap "Create account", fill the form, and wait
for your branch manager to approve. You'll be able to log in once they
do.

Staff: your branch manager will create your login for you.

ABOUT US

AkvoPura is a Pakistani-owned mineral water business in Timergara and
Shergarh, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Contact: akvopura4@gmail.com.
```
